How opt-out works

When you click "Opt Out", this site sets a single functional cookie named statless_opt_out with a 1-year lifespan solely to remember your choice. No tracking data or personal identifiers are stored in this cookie. If you clear your browser cookies, you will need to opt out again.

Alternatively, enabling Global Privacy Control (GPC) in your browser (e.g. Brave, Firefox, or privacy extensions) will automatically opt you out on every visit without requiring a cookie.
