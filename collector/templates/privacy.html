1. Lawful Basis & Purposes of Processing (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))

Processing is carried out under Legitimate Interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). The operator pursues the legitimate interest of measuring readership volume, coarse geographical distribution, referring platforms, and dwell time across published content. No persistent user profiles, behavioral tracking, cross-site identity resolution, or advertising operations are conducted.

3. What is Never Collected or Stored

Raw IP addresses, tracking cookies, persistent device identifiers, query strings or URL paths from referrers, mouse movements, clicks, keystrokes, or scroll coordinates are never collected or stored. Raw User-Agent strings are classified at the ingestion boundary and immediately discarded.

4. Retention & Storage Limitation (GDPR Art. 5(1)(e))

Tracked events are automatically and permanently deleted after {{ retention_days }} days via an automated daily pruning job. Ephemeral cryptographic salts rotate every {{ salt_rotate_hours }} hours, breaking re-identification across windows.

5. Your Rights & GDPR Article 11(2)

Under GDPR Chapter III, you have the rights to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21).

Application of GDPR Article 11(2): Because this service does not store raw IP addresses, cookies, or persistent identifiers, and hashes rotate every {{ salt_rotate_hours }} hours, the controller is unable to identify any individual visitor from the stored records. In accordance with Article 11(2) of the GDPR, individual access, rectification, or erasure requests generally cannot be fulfilled unless you provide additional information that uniquely links your device to a specific log entry within an active rotation window.

Operators can hard-delete all stored events for an entire page key using the document lifecycle purge endpoint.

6. Opt-Out Options (Right to Object, Art. 21)

You can object to all processing and prevent any visits from being counted through any of the following methods:

  • Direct Web Opt-Out: Visit the Visitor Opt-Out Page to toggle your tracking preference. This sets a single functional cookie strictly to remember your opt-out choice.
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC): Any request carrying the Sec-GPC: 1 HTTP header is dropped immediately before recording.
  • Do Not Track (DNT): Any request carrying DNT: 1 is dropped immediately before recording.
  • Header / Query Flag: Sending X-Opt-Out: 1 or appending ?optout=1 to any tracked URL prevents logging.

7. Right to Lodge a Complaint (GDPR Art. 13(2)(d))

If you believe that the processing of personal data relating to you infringes the GDPR or applicable national data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU/EEA Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement (or the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK).

8. Access to Statistics & Data Security

Aggregate counts for a document are accessible via the stats API. Deployments should be secured behind HTTPS and protected with an access token (STATS_TOKEN) to prevent unauthorized public indexing of site metrics in accordance with GDPR Article 25(2) (Data Protection by Default) and Article 32 (Security of Processing).
